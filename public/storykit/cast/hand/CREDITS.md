# Hand-built cast: credits

`young-hero.glb` (`hand/young-hero`, "Mateo"): an original Story Kit character, an invented person with no real
likeness. Built by the Character Forge from its young-hero anchor (MakeHuman's base body, clothes and hair, CC0 1.0),
then polished by hand (skin and stubble paint, shell hair, hands, face shape, cloth).
His moves come from the Story Kit's clip pack (`cast/cast-clips.glb`), clips from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture
Database.

`old-boss.glb` (`hand/old-boss`, "Augustin"): an original Story Kit character, an invented person with no real likeness.
Built by the Character Forge from its old-boss anchor (MakeHuman's base body, clothes and hair, CC0 1.0), then polished
by hand (trimmed silver beard, bald dome and horseshoe fringe, weathered skin maps, hands,
brows, irises, aged teeth, lips, a repp-stripe tie, collar and lapel edges, flatter jacket cloth, rounded shoulders). His
moves come from the Story Kit's clip pack (`cast/cast-clips.glb`), clips from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database.
