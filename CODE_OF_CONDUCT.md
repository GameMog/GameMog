# Code of Conduct

MOG CITY is a community project, and we want everyone who plays it, files an issue or sends a pull request to feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about code and ideas, not about people.
- Welcome newcomers. Everybody's first pull request was somebody's first pull request.
- Give feedback that helps someone improve their work, and take feedback the same way.
- Credit other people's work.

## What isn't OK

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Hate or discrimination of any kind, including about identity, background, age, body or ability.
- Sexual content or advances.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, scams, or using the project to sell anything or to promote stocks, investments or trading schemes. The game jokes about banks; this space doesn't shill anything.

## Where it applies

It applies wherever Mog City is worked on: its `mogcity` branch of GameMog/GameMog, and the issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews and commits about it, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request. That goes to GitHub's own moderators.

You can also open an issue titled **"Conduct"**, without details, and the GameMog team will contact you to arrange a private channel. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens

The GameMog team may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code. The response depends on how serious the behaviour is.

This code draws on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
