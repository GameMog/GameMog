### Original to this game

"We All Gonna Make It", "Number Go Up" and "Fifth Ave Glide" play on WAGMI FM (`wagmi-theme.m4a`, `wagmi-pump.m4a`,
`wagmi-glide.m4a`). They were composed as code in `wagmi/radio/build/music/` and are MIT-licensed with the rest of the
game.

## Characters

Dan, Gears, Jane, Fiona and Mr. Big (`assets/characters/*.glb`) are original characters made for Mog City (invented
people) from [MakeHuman](http://www.makehuman.org)'s base body, clothes and hair (CC0 1.0; Jane and Mr. Big from
GameMog's CC0 MakeHuman people pack; Mr. Big's glasses, cigar and study are made in code, `wagmi/mission/mrbig.js`). Their everyday clips come from the
[CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database](http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu) (free for all uses; the data itself may not be
resold; created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217). Dan's freeflow fight moves are Spiderbench's (see below).

## Engine sounds

Dan's car (`assets/sfx/engine/v8-*.m4a`): loops cut at five engine speeds from "Maserati GranTurismo S Exhaust" by
lmartins ([Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maserati_GranTurismo_S_Exhaust.ogg), originally
[Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/lmartins/sounds/465453/)), under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Trimmed, pitch-flattened, looped, levelled and re-encoded.
The gearbox, shifts and pops (`wagmi/audio/supercar.js`) are original.

## Crowd sounds

The street crowd's walla (`assets/sfx/city/crowd-walla.m4a`) is cut and mixed from five takes of
**"GameSoundCon2024 Walla XY-AKG214-96k"** by ShangusBurger (Shane Vincent),
[Freesound pack 42098](https://freesound.org/people/ShangusBurger/packs/42098/), under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): Conference Room Walla American English
[01](https://freesound.org/s/763895/) and [02](https://freesound.org/s/763896/),
[Soft Conference Room Walla American English Library](https://freesound.org/s/763898/),
[Sparse Chatter and Laughter](https://freesound.org/s/763848/) and
[Coughs, Chatter and Laughs](https://freesound.org/s/763851/). CC0 needs no credit; we give it anyway. We reduced the
room's reverb, equalised the takes to sound outdoors and a little distant, levelled them, narrowed the stereo, joined
three into a seamless 37.5 s loop (with the two sparse takes mixed under it) and re-encoded it to AAC.

## The city

The city is built on Slop New York by Mr.doob, under the MIT License (see [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE)). Some city code is
ported from Slop New York's newer commits by Mr.doob, under the same MIT License; each ported part names its commit in a
short header comment.

The map data: Central Park's features, the piers, the landmarks' footprints and the New Jersey shore are
© [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), under the
[ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/); the borough line, the street centrelines and the parks are
from [NYC Open Data](https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us) (NYC DCP Borough Boundaries, NYC Street Centerline (CSCL), NYC
Parks Properties).

## Voices

Dan's, Jane's, Knox's, Mack's, Gears', Fiona's, Brick's and Dash's lines are synthesised with
[Qwen3-TTS](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS) by the Qwen team, Alibaba Cloud (Apache-2.0): each voice was designed
once from a written description and every line cloned from it, run with [mlx-audio](https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-audio)
(MIT). Mr. Big's are synthesised with [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) by hexgrad (Apache-2.0),
its stock voice bm_lewis, through kokoro-onnx (MIT), heard as it is. The voices are invented. Any character's lines not
yet voiced are placeholders made with the macOS speech voices; `NOTICE` explains their terms, and `docs/VOICE-LINES.md` explains how to replace them.
