# Vantor GT: downloaded body

`vantor-gt.glb` is based on **"Porsche GT3 RS"** by **Black Snow**
(https://sketchfab.com/BlackSnow02), from https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/porsche-gt3-rs-e738eae819c34d19a31dd066c45e0f3d,
licensed under **CC BY 4.0** (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). `LICENSE.txt` is the notice that came with it.

Credit line (keep it wherever the car is shown):

> Vantor GT body based on "Porsche GT3 RS" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/porsche-gt3-rs-e738eae819c34d19a31dd066c45e0f3d)
> by Black Snow (https://sketchfab.com/BlackSnow02), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Modified.

Changed from the original: every badge and crest mesh removed (wing, tail, wing uprights, bumper, front lid), the
crest and model-lettering textures blanked, the hidden mechanicals (suspension, engine, exhaust runs, tanks, pedals)
removed, the rest simplified with meshoptimizer (305k to 110k triangles), meshopt-compressed. In the game it is repainted
(wagmi/storyrun/vantor.js) and carries the invented name "VANTOR GT". The game's car is a look-alike: it bears no maker's
name, crest, badge or model lettering, and nothing in Mog City claims any connection with any car maker. CC BY 4.0
covers the modeller's copyright only.
