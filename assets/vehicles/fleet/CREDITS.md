# Town cars: realistic models, CC BY 4.0

The cars, vans, trucks and buses of a Hometown town (its traffic, the cars parked along its curbs, the car a player
takes, a town's own default car and its police in a chase) are made from the models below, each licensed under
**CC BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The adapted files in this folder are shared under the same
licence; they are **not** covered by the MIT License in `LICENSE`. `fleet.json` records every file's SHA-256 and size,
and each model's source download (its SHA-256, size and date).

## What was changed (all of them: modified)

- Daniel Zhabotinsky's cars: every badge mesh removed, and the shared badge texture atlas is not shipped at all; the
  empty badge mounts and the lettering and emblems cut as geometry removed (a hatchback's grille name, a cab's hood and
  trunk crests, an old sedan's hood ornament); the number plates' characters and state names removed (blank plates);
  the tyres' sidewall lettering (in the colour and the occlusion-roughness textures), a lamp's maker-like name plaque, a stray word on the underbody texture and the glass templates' labels cleaned off; the
  cab's livery cleared of every sticker (a company name, slogans, ads) and the cruiser's livery cut down to a plain
  POLICE (its unit number, slogan, flags and the 911 panel removed). The parts were re-oriented and re-centred to the
  game's frame, merged onto one shared texture atlas (`kit*.webp`), their textures downscaled and WebP-encoded, two
  simplified levels of detail added, and the geometry meshopt-compressed. The paint is the game's own per car.
- Comrade1280's packs: split into their vehicles (the ones the game uses are here); the civil pack's wheels split from
  the bodies by geometry; re-oriented and re-centred; textures downscaled (normal maps dropped) and WebP-encoded; the
  lamps tagged; two simplified levels of detail added; geometry meshopt-compressed. Their liveries' words are generic
  (POLICE, TAXI, AMBULANCE, SCHOOL BUS, POSTAL SERVICE, ROAD SERVICE, FIRE DEPARTMENT).
- Names: the files use generic names (`gt95`, `sedan10`...) and the game only ever calls a car by its kind (a sports
  car, a coupe, a pickup); the models' own titles appear only in these credits.

The cars are look-alikes with invented names and no maker's badge, crest, name or lettering: nothing in Mog City claims
any connection with any car maker, and CC BY 4.0 covers the modellers' copyright only.

Built with `tools/vehicles/build-fleet.mjs` from the raw downloads (never committed).
