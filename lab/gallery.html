The gallery (Mission 4)

Walk it on the Seaport map with Dan's normal controls (WASD or the touch stick, Space to jump, hold F to act). Each link opens the game straight at that point, with the gallery up on South St (wagmi/mission/m4-gallery.js install).

    The clue: Rook says to look at the statue's reflection; from the brass medallion the black glass shows the mark on the back of its crown, and the frame whose cartouche wears that mark holds the High Artwork. A wrong piece trips the alarm and fails it (retried from the door with a fresh deal); add &wrong=alarm for the alarm only.

    Add &time=twilight for the blue hour, &sigil=star|moon|eye to fix the statue's mark. Any query given to this page is passed on (?go=clue opens the clue straight away).
