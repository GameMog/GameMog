# SF 2040 — story & gameplay design (story2 lane)

## Logline
Forty-one days after the asteroid Erebus split the Presidio open and let the Bio-Preserve's creatures into the city,
ex-park ranger Cal Mercer rides his horse Ranger across a wild San Francisco to find his wife and daughter, and learns
the animals are not loose by accident: someone is steering them.

## The world in one paragraph
Erebus broke up over the Pacific; one piece came down on the Presidio and the Bio-Preserve's walls came down with it.
The Preserve was a gene-rescue park: rebuilt animals and engineered "hybrids", each wearing a containment collar that
answers to a shepherd tone, broadcast by the Preserve's security mech, WARDEN-7. The Preserve's old security
contractors kept their armour and became the **Iron Saints**, led by **Deacon Vale**. They found WARDEN-7, parked it on the
roof of the hospital on Hyde St, and use its tone to drive creatures at any camp that will not pay them "the tithe":
food, medicine, horses. The red glow of the crater sits to the west over everything.

## Three acts

**Act I — The call (M1, M2).** Day 41. At the Embarcadero camp Hollis, who runs the radio, plays Cal a three-day-old
fragment: Nina, Market and Church, medicine. The camp is starving, so Cal earns the ride: he scavenges food along Market
St and meets the Iron Saints, and Vale on the open band ("That's Saint property, rider"). At Market & Church he walks
Ranger, silent, past the Brood Mother's sleeping nest, finds medicine and Nina's first note: she and June are moving
patients to the hospital on Hyde St, and the creatures' collars "are still transmitting". The Brood Mother wakes as he
leaves. *Stakes:* the camp's survival and the first proof Nina and June are alive.

**Act II — The shepherd (M3, M4).** The camp must move before Vale's tithe night, and it needs horses. At Civic Center
Old Silver, a collared silverback, holds a wild herd on City Hall's steps. Cal brings him down, finds June's backpack
(her inhaler gone: she has the spare; a page of Nina's notebook: "WARDEN-7 is broadcasting from Hyde St. Somebody turned
it back on") and drives the herd into the camp's corral at UN Plaza. **The turn:** at the hospital WARDEN-7 is no relic,
it is the Saints' weapon. Cal destroys it, then has ninety seconds to cut the roof relays before the tone pulls every
creature for a mile onto the block. At the core he makes **the choice**: *smash it* (the tone dies forever; the creatures
go wild and free) or *send them home* (one last tone, calling every collar back to the Preserve). **The reveal:** Vale
breaks in on the band: "Ask your radio man who opened the Preserve's gates." Hollis confesses: he was the Preserve's
night keeper, and when the fires came he opened every gate so the animals would not burn. Everything loose in the city
is his mercy gone wrong.

**Act III — Home (M5, M6).** The chalk on the hospital doors sends Cal to the grey house on Washington St by Lafayette
Park. He climbs the park blowing the ranger whistle he gave June (three short blasts: "I'm here"), and follows three faint
answers up the hill; the whistle wakes The Matriarch. He leads her away from the house at a gallop down the hill, fights
her on the flat, and climbs back to the reunion. At dawn the Mercers ride back to the camp just as Vale comes for it with
everything he has left. Cal holds the camp through three waves and puts Vale down in front of the people he robbed.
Hollis asks to be forgiven; Cal gives him Ranger's reins to hold. **Payoff:** a family together, a camp that owes no
one, and a radio man who keeps his post. **Post-credits hook:** night over the crater; WARDEN-7's voice from a dead
speaker in the Presidio: "Enclosure B. Seal failure." Something far larger moves in the red.

## Characters and arcs
- **Cal Mercer** (hero). Ex-park ranger; keeps animals alive for a living and now has to fight them. *Arc:* from a man
  who rides alone and trusts no one ("I keep riding") to one who comes home and forgives Hollis. His tell: he talks to
  Ranger, not to people.
- **Ranger** (the horse bond). Tutorial partner; Cal's lines to him are the game's quiet beats ("Easy. Easy."). Paid off
  by June ("You kept Ranger?" / "He kept me.") and the act-end quote.
- **Dr. Nina Mercer.** ER doctor who never stopped working: moving patients, leaving notes. Heard (radio, notes) before
  she is seen. *Arc:* from voice to presence; in M6 she runs the camp's aid tent under fire.
- **June Mercer** (13). Asthmatic, stubborn, smarter than the adults. Present through objects: backpack, inhaler,
  whistle. *Arc:* from someone Cal must rescue to someone who saves him (she calls the waves from the radio tower in M6).
- **Hollis.** Runs the camp radio. Warm, tired, guilty. *Arc:* the reveal in M4 (he opened the gates); asks forgiveness
  in M6; keeps the post.
- **Deacon Vale** (antagonist). Leader of the Iron Saints, ex-Preserve security. *Motive:* order on his terms, "a city
  that pays its shepherd". Calm, polite, never shouts. Heard on the band from M1; met in M6 as the last wave's boss.
- **Pike** (ally). A young Saint Cal knocks down and leaves alive in M1. Radios Cal in M4 where the relays are, and warns
  the camp in M6 that Vale is coming. Shows mercy returned.
- **WARDEN-7** (the machine). Speaks only containment protocol on its loudspeaker. Its last line is the hook.

Speakers (subtitle ids): Cal, Nina, June, Hollis, Radio (Nina's broken broadcast), Vale, Pike, Warden.

## The glue
- **Travel:** every ride has radio on the band (Hollis, Vale, Pike) timed into the ride, ambient wildlife (wild horses,
  hybrids that flee, a herd crossing Market) and street-name place cards; a quiet line from Cal to Ranger when the
  radio is silent.
- **Objective clarity:** every step has HUD objective text with its noun coloured, a GPS route and a beam/corona, blips on
  the radar; new steps show their own meter (NOISE, SIGNAL, HERD, WAVE).
- **Checkpoints + fail/retry:** a checkpoint on every playable step; a fail says why and gives a tip (stealth: "Slow to a
  walk (Q) near them"); Retry goes back to the checkpoint, Skip after three fails.
- **Continue:** the last checkpoint is saved; reloading the page offers CONTINUE into the mission at that checkpoint;
  Mission select (Esc) replays any passed mission.
- **Pacing:** fights are followed by a quiet beat (a ride with radio, a cutscene, a camp beat); no two fights back to
  back without a ride or a scene between.
- **Tutorial woven in:** M1 teaches F mount, W/Shift/Q gaits, Space jump, J strike from the saddle, each when first
  needed, never as a wall of text.
- **Difficulty a human can beat:** boss fights tuned with the player's health and the boss's phases; tested with a
  "fair" bot that does not heal or finish.
- **Ending:** reunion (warm grade), dawn at the camp, the act quote, STORY COMPLETE, the title, then the post-credits coda.

## Rules kept
Original names and places only; the park is the Presidio Bio-Preserve; no real people; family-level intensity (creatures
are knocked down, people are "down", nobody dies on screen); no music.

## Playtest log (11 Nov, headless Chrome, test drive at real pace `&realpace=1`, fair fights `&fair=1`)
- Autopilot, every mission in turn (M1..M6 + coda): passes, 0 page errors. Test-drive times (legs jumped): M1 2:30,
  M2 1:50, M3 2:40, M4 2:20 (new core scene), M5 1:00, M6 1:00 + coda.
- Real pace / fair bot (no health top-up, no finishing blow; it only rides, presses J and steers):
  M3 herd drive 1:34; M4 WARDEN-7 + hybrid won in 0:26 (hp 200), relays under the clock in 0:16, M4 from the fight to
  the end 2:40; M5 the chase 1:33, The Matriarch won in 1:37 with 104/220 left (was lost at 900 hp: tuned to 520);
  M6 three waves won on foot in 2:00; the M2 sneak about 4 min at the bot's careful pace (the GPS takes a side street).
- Fixed from playtests: the opening stood Cal on the plaza that reads as water (camp spot and facing moved, people
  staged behind him among the tents); a fight that started 151 m out failed at once ('You left the fight'); the
  sneak's sleepers on the route's lane; the Matriarch stuck behind a block (now follows his trail) and cleared from
  her own fight; creatures crashing the horse's siege code (bangCar); a marching wave never woke for a mounted rider;
  the herd step started past its herd; M4's choice staged at the wreck with a prompt that says what each option does.
- Still open: the plaza lawns (Sue Bierman Park, Lafayette Park) read as water in some light (world lane); the GPS
  routes the horse round one-way streets (the M2 sneak's detour); the saddle fight's balance vs a human is untested by
  hand; WARDEN-7's wreck may already have dissolved when the core scene plays.
