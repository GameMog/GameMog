Voices

Every spoken line, rendered on this computer. Lines without a clip play as subtitles.

Reading the pack...

Voices: Kokoro-82M by hexgrad (Apache-2.0), downloaded from Hugging Face and run by transformers.js (Apache-2.0) and ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Phonemes: eSpeak NG as phonemizer.js, a separate unmodified file under the GPL-3.0-or-later (its licence and source). Synthetic voices: no real person's voice is used.
